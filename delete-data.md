# Delete your data

Coffee Quitter is designed for privacy. Most of your data — drinks logged,
taper schedules, app settings — is stored only on your device and is never
sent to any server. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Data stored on your device

To delete it: uninstall Coffee Quitter from your device, or open
**Settings → Apps → Coffee Quitter → Storage → Clear data** on Android.

## Subscription data held by RevenueCat

If you have purchased Coffee Quitter Pro, our subscription processor
([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/)) holds:

- An anonymous subscriber identifier
- Your purchase history for the app

To request deletion of this data, email **coffeequitter.support@gmail.com**
with the subject "Delete my Coffee Quitter data" and include your Google
Play purchase email so we can identify the account. We will action the
request within 30 days and confirm by email.

## Account data

Coffee Quitter has no account system. There is no email address, name, or
login credential stored anywhere.

## Questions

Email **coffeequitter.support@gmail.com** for any data-related question.
